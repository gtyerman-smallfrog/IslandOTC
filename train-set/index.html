🚂 Island Rail

A 3D train set. Build the railway, control the traffic, deliver the goods.

Your job

  • Three trains are waiting in the holding yard next to Central Station.
  • Lay track from the station to the Harbour, the Quarry and Hilltown. Every delivery between places pays out.
  • Track that crosses a road becomes a level crossing with boom gates. Track through a hill becomes a tunnel (they cost more!).
  • Trains obey the track controls: they stop at red signals, follow the switches as they are set, wait for trains ahead and slow for curves. Two trains on the same tile is a crash.

Controls

  • 2 Lay Track: drag across the ground. Drag off an existing line to create a switch; cross a line at right angles for a diamond crossing. Click a piece to rotate it.
  • 3 Signal: click near the end of a track piece to place a signal (starts red). Click a signal any time to toggle red / green.
  • 1 Select: click a switch to throw it, click a train to open its control panel and send it somewhere.
  • 4 Erase: remove track or signals (half refund).
  • Camera: left-drag orbit, right-drag or Shift+drag pan, wheel zoom, W A S D move, Q/E rotate.
  • On a touchscreen: one finger orbits (or lays track), two fingers pinch to zoom and drag to move around the island. The + and − buttons zoom as well, and 🚂 opens the train list.
